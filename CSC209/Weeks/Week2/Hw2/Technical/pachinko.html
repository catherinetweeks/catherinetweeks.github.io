Pachinko

Min Jin Lee

2017

My review:

I absolutely loved this book. I loved the characters (especially Sunja and Isak), the reoccuring metaphors (dirt or ink underneath fingernails that couldn't be washed out), and even the writing style and pace. I did enjoy the first half of the book more than the last half, as I didn't really care for Solomon as a character as much as I did Sunja. Nonetheless I read this in just a couple days and enjoyed every minute.
