Sequoia National Forest Fun Facts

Giant Sequoia Tree Facts

  • The latin name for giant sequoia trees is: Sequoiadendron giganteum
  • The largest giant sequoia tree is called the General Sherman Tree. This tree is between 2,200 and 2,700 years old, is 275 feet tall and more than 100 feet around!

Sequoia National Park Facts

  • Sequoia National Park was founded on September 25th, 1890.
  • The park contains the highest point in the contiguous United States (Mount Whitney) at over 14,500 above sea level.
